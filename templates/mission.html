LEARN THE ALGORITHM

What is Binary Search?

Binary Search is an efficient searching algorithm that works on a sorted collection of elements. It repeatedly divides the search area into two halves.

01

Find the Middle

Check the middle element of the sorted array.

02

Compare the Value

Compare the middle element with your target.

03

Reduce the Search

Continue searching in the correct half.

04

Find the Target

Repeat until the target is found or no elements remain.
